Build an AI Chatbot

The Problem

As part of my weekend routine, I go grocery shopping on Saturdays and meal prep on Sundays. Lately, I have been struggling to figure out what I would like to eat throughout the week, and wasting too much time trying out new recipes. So I decided to design an AI Chatbot that, based on my preferences, creates a weekly meal plan.

The Process

I created the chatbot using Mizou, and due to the free plan limitations, a couple of the features that were part of my original idea couldn't be implemented. The text script I used to train the chatbot contained the following questions:

  1. Ask for the user's location to find the nearest grocery store and price out the ingredients once the plan is done. (Unavailable feature)
  2. Do you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions?
  3. Do you follow a certain diet?
  4. What are your daily macros?
  5. Do you have any food preferences? Is there something you dislike?
  6. How many meals would you like to have a day?

The answers to questions 1–6 should be stored so the user won't have to answer them every interaction. From there, the bot will proceed to ask the next questions:

  1. What kind of foods are you in the mood for this week?
  2. What's your budget?
  3. Are there any changes to answers 1–6? If so, address them. Otherwise, create the meal plan.
  4. Once the meal plan is created, generate a PDF file with the menu, grocery list, and total cost. (Unavailable Feature)

Important: After the first interaction, if there haven't been any changes reported in step 9, the assistant must start future chats by prompting questions 7–9.

The Result

As someone who has previously used AI to create a personalized meal plan, I concluded that my chatbot is a beginner-level application that does not produce results as thorough as those of more powerful AI tools I have used. However, it was still able to provide a basic meal plan.
